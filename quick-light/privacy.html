PRIVACY POLICY

Pocket Ray Privacy Policy

Updated: 29 October 2026

1. Core app use

Pocket Ray (Android package com.vixora.quick_light) does not require a Vixora account for its core flashlight experience. The app uses device capabilities such as the LED torch, display brightness and screen-awake controls. Basic preferences may be stored locally.

2. Information you do not need to provide

Vixora does not require your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location or payment information to use the core torch, screen-light and SOS functions.

3. Advertising and Yandex

The currently published version may be ad-free. Vixora is testing Yandex Mobile Ads in developer builds. An approved future version may show a small banner supplied by the Yandex Advertising Network and its authorized demand partners. When ads are enabled, their SDK may process network connection information and permitted device identifiers to deliver, measure and protect advertising. The current Android integration removes the advertising ID permission, does not pass device location to Yandex and does not make live ad requests while production approval and user privacy controls are pending. Review Yandex's Privacy Policy.

4. Local storage

App settings may be stored on your device. Clearing app storage or uninstalling Pocket Ray may remove those preferences.

5. Internet, diagnostics and external pages

Core lighting functions are designed to work offline. The app includes Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics for product-use measurement and troubleshooting; these services may process installation identifiers, app activity, device information and crash diagnostics. Internet access may also be used for approved advertising and when opening Vixora's support, privacy or terms pages.

6. Privacy choices

Android privacy and permission settings remain available. For versions with advertising, required consent, rejection and withdrawal options must be available before live ads are activated in relevant regions. The developer test build does not enable production ad requests. To ask about available privacy choices or to exercise applicable rights, contact Vixora using the details below.

7. Security and retention

Vixora does not require an account to use Pocket Ray's lighting features and minimizes direct data collection. Third-party providers maintain their own security safeguards and retention policies. See the provider privacy notices linked below for more detail.

8. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when Pocket Ray's features, SDKs or legal requirements materially change. For privacy questions, use the Vixora contact page.

Data controller and contact

Vixora publishes Pocket Ray. Contact vixora.tech@gmail.com for privacy questions or applicable data requests. Include the app name; never send passwords, payment details or verification codes by email.

Purposes, legal bases and service providers

The torch, screen-light, SOS features and local preferences are primarily handled on-device. Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics may process app-use events, installation identifiers, crash reports and device diagnostics for service quality, troubleshooting and security. Yandex Mobile Ads, when enabled, may process network and permitted device information to deliver and measure ads. Pocket Ray does not request precise location for advertising, and the current SDK configuration disables location tracking and removes the Android advertising ID permission. Where GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on applicable lawful bases for necessary app functions and proportionate diagnostics; advertising consent will be requested where required before enabling live ads, with controls to withdraw it. Details about third-party processing are available from Yandex and Firebase.

Retention and international processing

On-device preferences may remain until you clear app storage or uninstall the app, subject to device backup behavior. Firebase and, when active, Yandex use their respective retention arrangements for diagnostics and advertising data. Processing by service providers may occur in other countries under their applicable data-transfer arrangements. Contact Vixora for questions about a specific retention period or request.

Your rights and requests

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of personal data, object to certain processing or withdraw consent for consent-based features. You may also complain to your relevant data-protection authority. To submit a request, contact Vixora using the address above; some actions involving data stored only on your device can be performed by clearing the app's data in Android settings.
