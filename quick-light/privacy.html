Quick Light Privacy Policy

Effective: 18 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Vixora handles information when you use Quick Light (Android package com.vixora.quick_light).

1. Core app use

Quick Light does not require a Vixora account. Its core flashlight features use device capabilities such as the LED torch, screen brightness and screen-awake controls. Basic app preferences may be stored locally on your device so settings can be remembered.

2. Core functionality and personal information

Vixora does not require you to provide a name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location or payment information to use the core flashlight, brightness and SOS features.

3. Advertising

Quick Light includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK and may display advertising. Google Mobile Ads may automatically process information such as IP address, general location inferred from IP, app interactions, diagnostic information and device or advertising identifiers for advertising, analytics, measurement and fraud-prevention purposes. Google processes this information under its own privacy terms and applicable consent requirements.

4. Local storage

App preferences may be saved locally on your device. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app may remove those settings.

5. Internet access and external pages

The core lighting functions are designed to work without an internet connection. Internet access may be used for advertising and to open Vixora information, privacy, terms or support pages. Website and advertising providers may receive ordinary technical request information such as IP address and browser or device details.

6. Third-party services

Quick Light currently uses Google Mobile Ads. If Vixora adds analytics, crash reporting, cloud services or other third-party SDKs in a future release, this policy and the applicable store disclosures will be updated to reflect those changes.

7. Your choices

You can use Android privacy and advertising controls to manage advertising identifiers and related preferences. Where required, advertising consent or privacy choices may also be presented by Google's advertising services. You can remove locally stored Quick Light preferences by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.

8. Security and retention

Vixora aims to minimize direct collection of personal information in Quick Light. Information processed by third-party services is retained according to those providers' policies and configurations. No digital service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes

Vixora may update this Privacy Policy when Quick Light's features, SDKs or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the current published version.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, use the Vixora contact page.
