COLOR BY NUMBER · IN DEVELOPMENT

Find your calm in color.

Color Haven is Vixora's offline-friendly color-by-number experience with genuine playable artwork, precise zoom-and-tap coloring, daily art, favorites, achievements, Find Missing Part and completion time-lapse sharing.

Explore Features

22 playable starter artworks

The current milestone includes 22 genuinely playable artworks across multiple categories, with content expanded only when each item is validated.

Precision coloring

Use numbered palettes with remaining counts, pinch to zoom, pan freely and tap accurately into small regions.

Find Missing Part

Locate hard-to-spot unfinished areas. In ads-enabled builds, optional rewarded ads may be used for this convenience feature.

Autosave & favorites

Coloring progress, preferences and favorites are saved locally so you can return to unfinished artwork.

Daily art & achievements

Daily artwork, achievements and basic statistics add lightweight progression without requiring an account.

Completion time-lapse

Create a replay of your coloring progress when an artwork is completed and share exported results using your device's share options.

Explore & My Art

Browse and search artwork, manage your completed and in-progress pieces, and keep your creative library organised.

Light, dark & system themes

Choose the appearance that fits your device while keeping the artwork itself at the centre of the experience.

Firebase reliability tools

Analytics and crash reporting help Vixora understand product usage and diagnose stability issues as Color Haven develops.
