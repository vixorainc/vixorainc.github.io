PRIVACY POLICY

Color Haven Privacy Policy

Effective: 18 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Vixora handles information when you use Color Haven (Android package com.vixora.color_haven).

1. Offline-friendly core experience

Color Haven does not require a Vixora account for its core coloring experience. Artwork progress, favorites, preferences, achievements, statistics and similar app state are primarily stored locally on your device.

2. Information stored on your device

The app may save coloring progress, selected colors, favorites, theme preferences, daily-art state, achievement progress and other settings so that your experience can be restored. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app may remove this locally stored information.

3. Firebase Analytics

Color Haven uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand general app usage and improve the product. The app may log events such as app opens and interactions with features. Google Analytics for Firebase may process app-instance identifiers, device and app information, approximate region derived from network information, and event data according to Google's Firebase and privacy terms.

4. Firebase Crashlytics

Color Haven uses Firebase Crashlytics to identify and diagnose crashes and serious errors. Crashlytics may process crash stack traces, relevant app state, device metadata, app version information, installation identifiers and related diagnostics needed to provide crash-reporting services.

5. Advertising and consent

Color Haven includes Google Mobile Ads support. Advertising may be enabled in distributed builds. Before requesting ads, the app uses Google's consent information and privacy-choice mechanisms where required. When ads are enabled, Google Mobile Ads may process information such as IP address, general location inferred from IP, app interactions, diagnostic information and device or advertising identifiers for advertising, analytics, measurement and fraud-prevention purposes.

Depending on the build and feature, Color Haven may show interstitial or rewarded ads. A rewarded ad may be offered for optional convenience features such as Find Missing Part. Advertising availability and personalisation depend on consent, region, platform settings and Google's services.

6. Sharing and time-lapse exports

Color Haven can create completion images or time-lapse exports and can open the device's share interface when you choose to share. Vixora does not automatically upload your exported artwork to a Vixora server. Information shared to another app or service is handled according to the destination you select and that provider's privacy terms.

7. No Vixora account database

The current app does not require account registration or a Vixora cloud database for ordinary coloring progress. Vixora does not intentionally collect your individual coloring taps, favorites or unfinished artwork through a Vixora account service.

8. Children and general use

Color Haven does not require users to submit a name, email address or direct message to use the core coloring features. Store age settings, advertising configuration and other safeguards must remain consistent with the audience selected for each release. Vixora will update the app configuration and this policy if the target audience or data practices materially change.

9. Website hosting

The Color Haven website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub and ordinary web infrastructure may process technical request information such as IP address, browser details and security logs in connection with hosting the site.

10. Your choices

You can remove locally stored app progress by clearing Color Haven's storage or uninstalling the app. Android provides controls for advertising identifiers and related privacy settings. Where required, Color Haven may also provide access to Google's advertising privacy options.

11. Security and retention

Vixora aims to minimise data collection and use established service providers for analytics, crash reporting and advertising. Third-party providers retain information according to their own service terms, configurations and retention practices. No digital service can guarantee absolute security.

12. Changes

Vixora may update this Privacy Policy as Color Haven's features, SDKs or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the current published version.

13. Contact

For privacy questions, use the Vixora contact page.
