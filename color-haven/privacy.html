Color Haven Privacy Policy

Effective: 16 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Vixora handles information in Color Haven.

1. Offline-first design

Color Haven is designed so that its core coloring experience can work without creating an account or connecting to a Vixora backend. Coloring progress, favorites, preferences and similar app state are stored locally on the device in the current version.

2. Information Vixora currently collects

The current app build does not require a Vixora account, cloud database, paid API or Vixora analytics service, and Vixora does not intentionally receive your individual coloring taps, artwork progress or favorites from the app.

3. Device storage

The app may save progress, selected colors, favorites, theme and other settings on your device so that your experience can be restored. Removing the app or clearing its storage may remove this locally saved information.

4. Optional third-party services

Development builds may use a simulated rewarded-ad service rather than a live ad network. If Vixora later enables advertising, analytics, purchases, cloud backup or other third-party services, this policy will be updated before or alongside that functionality and the relevant providers may process information under their own privacy terms.

5. Website hosting

The Color Haven website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information in connection with hosting and securing the site under GitHub's own privacy practices.

6. Children

Color Haven is a general-audience coloring application. Vixora does not knowingly ask children to create an account or submit personal information through the current app experience. If future features change the way information is handled, appropriate safeguards and disclosures will be added.

7. Security

Vixora aims to minimize data collection and use secure, validated content and software practices. No digital service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes

Vixora may update this Privacy Policy as Color Haven evolves. The effective date on this page will be updated when material changes are published.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, use the Vixora contact page.
