PRIVACY POLICY

Huenara Privacy Policy

Updated: 29 October 2026

1. Core experience

Huenara (Android package com.vixora.color_haven) does not require a Vixora account for its core coloring experience. Coloring progress, favorites, preferences and similar state are primarily stored locally.

2. Local information

The app may save coloring progress, selected colors, favorites, theme preferences, daily-art state, achievement progress and other settings so your experience can be restored.

3. Firebase Analytics

Huenara uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand general app usage and improve the product. It may process app-instance identifiers, device/app information and event data according to Google's Firebase terms.

4. Firebase Crashlytics

Firebase Crashlytics may process crash traces, device metadata, app version information, installation identifiers and diagnostics needed to investigate stability issues.

5. Advertising and consent

Earlier Huenara builds may contain a legacy advertising SDK, but that does not mean ads are currently serving. Vixora is evaluating Yandex Mobile Ads as a replacement for a future approved release; Yandex is not yet confirmed as active in Huenara. Where implemented and approved, advertisements may include optional rewarded or interstitial formats. Advertising will only be enabled with the applicable consent, refusal and withdrawal controls, privacy disclosures and store declarations. When an approved Yandex integration is released, its partners may process network, device and permitted identifier data to deliver and measure ads. See Yandex's privacy information.

6. Sharing and exports

When you choose to share an exported artwork or completion result, Android's share interface may send it to the app or service you select. Vixora does not automatically upload your exported artwork to a Vixora server.

7. Your choices

You may clear local progress by clearing app storage or uninstalling Huenara. Android privacy settings and any advertising choices provided within your installed app version remain available. New advertising integrations require appropriate privacy choices before live activation.

8. Children and audience

Huenara does not require a name, email address or direct-message submission for core coloring. Store audience settings, advertising configuration and safeguards are kept aligned with each release.

9. Changes and contact

This policy is updated if Huenara materially changes its SDKs, advertising, analytics, cloud services or data practices. Contact Vixora through the support page.

Data controller and contact

Vixora publishes Huenara. Contact vixora.tech@gmail.com for privacy questions or applicable data requests. Include the app name; never send passwords, payment details or verification codes by email.

Purposes, legal bases and service providers

Coloring progress and preferences are normally stored on-device. Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics may process app interaction, installation, device and diagnostic information. Previously distributed versions may contain Google Mobile Ads components; their data practices are governed by the version installed and the provider's privacy terms. Any future approved Yandex Mobile Ads integration may process network and permitted device information for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Where GDPR or UK GDPR applies, necessary app features, proportionate diagnostics and optional advertising are handled using applicable lawful bases, with consent and withdrawal controls where required. Consult Firebase privacy information, Google's privacy policy for earlier builds, and Yandex's privacy policy for future Yandex-enabled releases.

Retention and international processing

On-device settings and progress may remain until you clear app storage or uninstall the app, subject to device backup behavior. Third-party analytics, advertising or authentication services use their configured retention arrangements; cloud data, if used, is retained only for the operating, security and legal purposes described in this policy. Processing by third-party providers may occur outside your country under their applicable data-transfer arrangements. For questions about a specific retention period, contact Vixora.

Your rights and requests

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of personal data, object to certain processing or withdraw consent for consent-based features. You may also complain to your relevant data-protection authority. To submit a request, contact Vixora using the address above; some actions involving data stored only on your device can be performed by clearing the app's data in Android settings.
