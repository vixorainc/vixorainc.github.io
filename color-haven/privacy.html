PRIVACY POLICY

Huenara Privacy Policy

Updated: 7 October 2026

1. Core experience

Huenara (Android package com.vixora.color_haven) does not require a Vixora account for its core coloring experience. Coloring progress, favorites, preferences and similar state are primarily stored locally.

2. Local information

The app may save coloring progress, selected colors, favorites, theme preferences, daily-art state, achievement progress and other settings so your experience can be restored.

3. Firebase Analytics

Huenara uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand general app usage and improve the product. It may process app-instance identifiers, device/app information and event data according to Google's Firebase terms.

4. Firebase Crashlytics

Firebase Crashlytics may process crash traces, device metadata, app version information, installation identifiers and diagnostics needed to investigate stability issues.

5. Advertising and consent

Huenara includes Google Mobile Ads support. Ads may include rewarded or interstitial formats where enabled. Consent and privacy-choice mechanisms are used where required before requesting ads.

6. Sharing and exports

When you choose to share an exported artwork or completion result, Android's share interface may send it to the app or service you select. Vixora does not automatically upload your exported artwork to a Vixora server.

7. Your choices

You may clear local progress by clearing app storage or uninstalling Huenara. Android and Google privacy controls can be used for applicable advertising choices.

8. Children and audience

Huenara does not require a name, email address or direct-message submission for core coloring. Store audience settings, advertising configuration and safeguards are kept aligned with each release.

9. Changes and contact

This policy is updated if Huenara materially changes its SDKs, advertising, analytics, cloud services or data practices. Contact Vixora through the support page.
