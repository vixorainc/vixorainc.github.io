PRIVACY POLICY

Tale Relay Privacy Policy

Updated: 7 October 2026

1. Accounts and authentication

Tale Relay uses account authentication to support contributions and saved activity. Depending on the sign-in method, Firebase Authentication and Google Sign-In may process identifiers such as account ID, email address and basic profile information supplied by the authentication provider.

2. Collaborative story content

Stories, branches and contributions you submit are stored so they can be shown to you and other users as part of the collaborative experience. Content you publish should be treated as information intended for community visibility within the product.

3. Cloud services

Tale Relay uses Firebase services such as Cloud Firestore and Cloud Functions for account-linked story data and trusted backend operations. Data is processed according to the app's configured Firebase region, security rules and Google's applicable terms.

4. Moderation and safety

Reports, moderation signals and relevant content may be processed to enforce community rules, investigate abuse and protect users and the service.

5. Analytics and crash diagnostics

Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics may process app-instance identifiers, app interactions, device/app metadata, crash traces and diagnostics to improve reliability and understand general usage.

6. No Firebase Storage in the current baseline

The current V1 architecture does not rely on Firebase Storage for ordinary story content and does not use Firebase Remote Config.

7. Retention and deletion

Account and story data may be retained while needed to provide the service, meet safety or legal obligations, resolve disputes and protect the integrity of collaborative content. Available deletion controls and support requests are handled subject to technical and legal constraints.

8. Security

Vixora uses authentication, security rules, validation and abuse-prevention measures, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Your choices

You may choose what story content to submit and can use available account, reporting and support options. Authentication-provider privacy controls may also apply.

10. Changes and contact

This policy will be updated when Tale Relay materially changes its account, cloud, moderation or data practices. Privacy questions can be sent through the Vixora contact page.
