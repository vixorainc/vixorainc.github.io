Remora Privacy Policy

Effective: 18 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Vixora handles information in Remora, an Android TV / Google TV remote application currently in development.

1. Local-network design

Remora is designed to discover, pair with and control compatible Android TV and Google TV devices over your local network. Remote-control commands are intended to travel between your phone and the paired TV on that local network rather than through a Vixora cloud backend.

2. No Vixora account or advertising

The current Remora development baseline does not include a Vixora account system, advertising, analytics, subscriptions or a Vixora backend service.

3. Device discovery and pairing information

To provide remote functionality, Remora may process local-network information such as compatible device addresses, service-discovery details, device names or identifiers, pairing state and connection information. This information is used to find and communicate with devices you choose to control.

4. Local security credentials

Remora uses a TLS client identity for secure pairing and control. Sensitive private-key material is stored using Android's secure keystore facilities where supported. Pairing and connection information may be stored locally on your device so you do not need to pair again every time.

5. Remote commands

Vixora does not intentionally receive a record of your ordinary remote-control commands through a Vixora server in the current architecture. Commands are used to control the paired device on the local network.

6. Permissions and network access

The app requires network capabilities needed to discover and connect to compatible TVs. Local-network behavior may expose ordinary network metadata to your router, TV and operating system as part of normal communication.

7. Website and support

If you open Vixora website, privacy, terms or support pages, the website host and your browser may process ordinary request information such as IP address and browser or device details.

8. Future changes

If Remora later adds analytics, crash reporting, advertising, accounts, cloud services or support for additional TV platforms, Vixora will update this policy and applicable store disclosures before or alongside those changes.

9. Security

Vixora uses local-network and platform security mechanisms intended to protect pairing and control data. No software or network can guarantee absolute security, and you are responsible for maintaining the security of your home network and devices.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, use the Vixora contact page.
