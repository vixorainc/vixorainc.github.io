Tiny Sway Privacy Policy

Updated: 7 October 2026

1. Floating overlay functionality

Tiny Sway uses Android's permission to display over other apps when you enable its floating charm feature. This permission allows the charm to remain visible above supported apps; it is not intended to read the content displayed by other apps.

2. Local preferences

Charm selection, size, position, cord or hanging preferences and similar customisation settings may be stored locally so your setup can be restored.

3. Analytics and crash diagnostics

Distributed builds may use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand feature usage, app stability and crashes. These services may process app-instance identifiers, device/app metadata, events and diagnostic information under Google's terms.

4. Advertising and consent

Tiny Sway may use Google Mobile Ads to support the free experience. Where required, Google's consent and privacy-choice mechanisms are used before ad requests. Google may process advertising identifiers, IP-derived general location, interactions and diagnostics.

5. Overlay interaction

Tiny Sway is designed so the charm itself can respond to touch while surrounding screen areas remain available to underlying apps where supported by Android.

6. Information not required for core use

Tiny Sway does not require you to submit contacts, photos, precise location or payment information to use the core charm customisation experience.

7. Your choices

You can disable overlay permission in Android settings, clear locally stored preferences, manage applicable advertising privacy choices or uninstall the app.

8. Changes and contact

This policy will be updated when Tiny Sway materially changes its permissions, SDKs or data practices. Questions can be sent through the Vixora contact page.
